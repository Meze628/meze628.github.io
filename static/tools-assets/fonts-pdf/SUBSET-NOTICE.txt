The sans/serif WOFF2 files are subsets of Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC renamed Meze Tools. Code font files are subsets of Cascadia Mono 2407.24 renamed Meze Code. Original outlines, metrics and copyright notices are retained under the corresponding SIL Open Font Licenses in this directory. Core Han characters use the first 1000 characters ranked by the kHanyuPinlu counts in Unicode Unihan 17.0.0; see tools-src/data/README.md and UNICODE-LICENSE.txt. Other supported characters are loaded in shards or from the complete original font. Regenerate with tools-src/build-fonts.py.
